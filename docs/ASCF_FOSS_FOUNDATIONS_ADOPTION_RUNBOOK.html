ASCF FOSS & Foundations Adoption Runbook

Verify Foundation/FOSS JAR versions between ASCF builds, then publish the HF to the Foundation portal

Adoption context

PurposePart 1: verify Foundation and FOSS JAR Implementation-Version adoption. Part 2: publish the HF on the Foundation site.

Part 1 — FOSS & Foundation JAR verification

Step 1

Start the comparison tool (Desktop shortcut or start_server.bat) and open http://localhost:5000/crm.

Step 2

Check the Build Comparison checkbox on the home page.

Step 3

Select ASCF only (do not select CRM/AMSS, UXF/OMNI, or DEX).

Step 4

Enter the previous ASCF HF build path in Old build and the new ASCF HF build path in New build.

Example: HF09 (old) → HF10 (new) on the ASCF release share.

Step 5

In ASCF comparison mode, select FOSS/Foundation (JAR Implementation-Version).

Step 6

For a Foundation JAR, choose Foundation product (e.g. Amdocs Core, Amdocs Security Manager) — the Jar name field fills automatically — or type the JAR name manually.

Step 7

For a FOSS library, choose Foss product (e.g. log4j-core.jar, commons-io.jar) — the Jar name field fills automatically — or type the JAR name manually.

Step 8

Click Run comparison and wait for the FOSS/Foundation HTML report to open.

Step 9

In the report, confirm Implementation-Version (old build) vs Implementation-Version (new build) and JAR paths match the expected adoption.

Step 10

Repeat steps 6–9 for each Foundation and FOSS JAR on your adoption checklist until all required libraries are verified.

Step 11

Optionally enable Environment Validation, enter SSH host/user/password and the remote ASCF deployment path, and validate Foundation/FOSS JAR Implementation-Version on the target environment.

Part 2 — Foundations site adoption

Step 12

With ASCF selected on the home page, click Foundations Link at the bottom of the page.

Step 13

In Step 1 (Prepare ASCF Foundation Link), select ASCF Product Version, enter the new HF number (2 digits), and click Prepare Foundation Link.

Example: version 10.24.0.pb01, HF 10.

Step 14

Confirm the prepare result shows the new download.html was created from the previous HF template.

Step 15

In Step 2 (Update Foundation Link), select the same ASCF Product Version, enter the current HF number, Month, Year, and ASCFDesignerUpdateSite creation date (YYYYMMDD), then click Update Foundation Link.

Example: month Jun, year 2026, creation date 20260609.

Step 16

Click Update Index.html, enter ASCF version, HF number, month, and year, then submit and confirm the index file was updated.

Step 17

Click Update Javadoc.html, enter ASCF Product Version and HF number, then submit and confirm the javadoc page was updated.

Step 18

Optionally click Update API manually and follow the on-screen links if API documentation must be updated for this HF.

Step 19

Return to the home page and click Administrative Tool (enabled when ASCF is selected).

Step 20

Enter New Hotfix Version (e.g. 10.24.0.pb01_hf10), Release Date, Download URL, and Release Notes URL, then click Submit & Launch Automation.

Step 21

In the opened Foundation admin browser window, verify the pre-filled hotfix form matches the new HF release details.

Step 22

Click Add Hotfix to publish the release on the Foundation site and confirm the new HF appears in the ASCF hotfix list.
